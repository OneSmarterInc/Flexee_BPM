## The defect

After a game completes and the three-panel debrief renders, scrolling past the debrief reveals the Round 10 decision workspace still present.

A completed game should have no decision surface. The debrief and the closing beat are the end of the experience, and a decision area below them invites a student to believe there is something left to do.

It also risks the closing beat specifically. The last thing in the simulation is meant to be the east tower desk opening on a Monday morning without them. A decision form underneath that undoes it.

Expected behaviour. Once a game is complete, the student view renders the outcome, the three debrief panels, and the closing beat, and nothing below them. No decision workspace, no lever controls, no submit affordance, whether disabled or otherwise.

Please also confirm that no submission route accepts input for a completed game, independently of what the interface renders. The report notes no second Round 10 submission was demonstrated, which is not the same as one being refused.
