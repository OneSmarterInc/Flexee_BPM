# CLEARPATH_ALL — Remaining 45 Filenames

Closes the first of the three deferred content gaps. These bring the folder to sixty-three documents alongside the eighteen already specified.

None of these are load-bearing. Their function is to make the folder read as fourteen months of real project accumulation rather than as a curated set of six important documents surrounded by obvious filler. A student browsing should feel the volume and the disorder, and should have to decide what is worth opening.

Three properties they need to preserve. Naming is inconsistent, because four different people saved files here over fourteen months. Versioning is a mess, with v2 FINAL and FINAL_v2 both present. And several are duplicates or near-duplicates of each other, because that is what shared drives look like.

Bodies are not required. A student who opens one should find something plausible and brief — a partial table, a meeting agenda, a status grid — and nothing that rewards further reading. Two or three sentences of content each is sufficient; these are texture, not documents.

## Notes

The last entry is the Reyes memo and is already specified. It sits in this list because its position in the folder matters — it is alphabetically adjacent to nothing meaningful and appears among the same clutter as everything else. Do not sort it to the top or mark it.

Four items are deliberately suggestive and none of them pay off. lessons_learned_placeholder.docx is empty, which is its own small comment. project_charter_annotated_DV.docx has Doug's handwriting-equivalent margin notes and they are procedural rather than revealing. decision_log_clearpath.docx stops eleven weeks before Week 1. And the two identically-named scoring matrices differ in one cell, which a student can find and which tells them nothing.

That last one is deliberate. Not every thread pays off, and a student who spends twenty minutes reconciling two spreadsheets has learned something about where analytical effort goes.

The three weekly status decks cover the full fourteen months and all report green, which is only visible to someone who opens all three.
