Annotate dates against the teaching calendar; none are assumed. Keep the same section game across weeks. Round names and tasks follow the existing decision workspace, not new instructional content.

## Operating rule

Teams submit their decisions. The instructor releases the next round when all teams have submitted, or uses the explicit missing-team override when appropriate. Only confirmed outstanding teams become recorded non-submissions under the accepted Phase 7 rules; accepted submissions remain intact. Only the instructor can correct a submitted decision while its round is still open. Release closes that opportunity for the old round.

Use **Refresh teams** to obtain current submission status without game reselection; release is manual, not scheduled by this table. See the [checklist](PRE_CLASS_INSTRUCTOR_CHECKLIST.md) and [operating note](INSTRUCTOR_PILOT_OPERATING_NOTE.md).
