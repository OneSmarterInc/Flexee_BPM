## Phase 6 correction note

The R9 basis-text check is a completeness heuristic, not a substantive assessment of financial support. Its existing implementation requires at least 40 characters and a match for a period/data-reference keyword (`day`, `week`, `month`, `quarter`, `year`, `period`, `data`, `report`, `queue`, `denial`, `registration`, or `baseline`); otherwise it halves the numeric basis score. It does not separately test punctuation. This behavior is unchanged. See `PHASE6_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md` for the lower ratio bands and always-available R10 explanation.
